About Eurotech Assessment & Certification

Eurotech (EACS) started operations in 2008 in Panchkula and has since expanded to a global scale. We provide accredited services including TIG & MIG Welder Training, AWS Welder Qualification, Eurocert ISO 9606-1 Certification, CE Marking, and Social Audits.

AWS & Eurocert Partnership

EACS is an exclusive Educational Company Member and International partner of the American Welding Society (AWS) Miami, USA. As the Official Representative of Eurocert, Greece (Notified Body 1128), we deliver welder qualification and ISO 9606-1 certification for TIG (GTAW) and MIG (GMAW) processes — aligned with EN ISO 15614-1, ASME Sec IX and AWS D1.1.

With talented professionals across offices in USA, UK, Canada, Middle East, Nigeria, and Ghana, and a nationwide presence in 10+ Indian cities, we bridge the gap between training and global placement. Our Mohali welding institute offers composite and process-specific TIG (GTAW) and MIG (GMAW) training with practical workshop sessions on real materials — preparing welders for oil & gas, fabrication, construction and offshore careers worldwide.
